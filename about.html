A LITTLE ABOUT THE BUILDER

Curiosity, craft
& iteration.

I'm Abdul Wahid, the person behind WahidSites. I enjoy turning ideas into usable digital experiences, learning by building and improving projects through real feedback.

How I approach a project

I start with the purpose: who the website is for, what it needs to communicate and what visitors should be able to do. Then I shape the layout, build the experience and refine it across screen sizes.

I value clarity, speed, practical functionality and design that feels intentional rather than overdecorated.

THE PROCESS

01 · Understand

Define the audience, goals and essential features.

02 · Design

Establish the visual direction, layout and content hierarchy.

03 · Build

Develop the pages and interactions with responsive behaviour.

04 · Refine

Test, polish and prepare the website for launch.
